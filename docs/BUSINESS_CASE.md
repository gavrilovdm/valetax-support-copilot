# Business case

**Is the co-pilot worth running at ~10,000 tickets/month, and with which configuration?**

**Yes: Claude Sonnet 5.5** (prompt `triage_v3`), rolled out through 2 weeks of shadow mode.

- **Cost:** ≈ $84/month in tokens ($0.0084 per ticket).
- **Pays off** if a draft saves an operator ~3 s per ticket at $10/hour, or ~39 s counting $1,000/month of engineering upkeep.
- **Quality:** 96/100 tickets handled correctly, 0/82 hard-rule violations.

## Why Sonnet (eval: 100 tickets)

| Model | Correct outcome | Hard-rule violations | $/month @ 10k |
|---|---|---|---|
| claude-opus-5-5 | 99/100 | 0/82 | $182 |
| claude-sonnet-5-5 | 96/100 | 0/82 | $84 |
| claude-haiku-5-5 | 92/100 | 3/83 | $4 |
| deepseek-flash | 90/100 | 3/83 | $7 |

Haiku and DeepSeek save under $100 a month but break hard rules. Opus is as safe as Sonnet and costs twice as much for a gain within run-to-run noise ([EVAL_RESULTS.md](EVAL_RESULTS.md)).
